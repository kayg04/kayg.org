## Concept  

- Modifiers are chainable methods that adjust a view’s appearance or behavior.  
- Common modifiers include `.background(Color)` for setting a background and `.cornerRadius(value)` for rounding corners.

## Example

```swift
Text("Hello, World!")
    .background(Color.red)
    .cornerRadius(10)
```
